Why local engineering matters

Oil & Gas, water and environmental operations in Algeria depend heavily on imported skids and services. The result is long delivery times, high maintenance costs and little room for customization.

Import dependence

Imported skids bring extra cost and long shutdowns when something fails.

Waste streams thrown away

Desalination brine and oily effluents are discarded instead of becoming usable products.

Operations without data

Without digital monitoring, failures are unplanned and energy use climbs.

Mission

Deliver practical, reliable and cost-effective industrial solutions by combining engineering, local manufacturing, automation, digital technology and field expertise.

Vision

What we build

Four lines of equipment, each configured to the customer’s flow, pressure and site conditions rather than sold as a fixed catalogue item.

Chemical injection skids

Dosing packages for corrosion inhibitor, biocide and other chemicals, with duty/standby pumps, redundant lines, instrumentation and PLC or SCADA integration.

Water treatment

RO and desalination packages, demineralization units, sand and activated-carbon filters, membrane cleaning systems and online conductivity and pH monitoring.

Pumping packages

Pumping units sized around the real workflow: flow, transfer time, fluid properties and pressure. Example: a 27 m³ fuel transfer unit targeting about 10 minutes, subject to hydraulic and safety validation.

Waste treatment

Oil waste units that recover reusable hydrocarbons from oily wastewater and sludge, and sabkha water treatment with brine valorization.
